# VoiceGuard — Data Provenance & Dataset Sourcing Reference

This document details the provenance, licensing, preprocessing, and ethical considerations for all audio and text datasets used to train and evaluate the VoiceGuard models.

---

## 1. Primary Acoustic Datasets

### 1.1 ASVspoof 2019 Logical Access (LA)
- **Source**: University of Edinburgh / EURECOM / Inria consortium.
- **Access / URL**: https://www.asvspoof.org/
- **License**: Creative Commons Attribution 4.0 International (CC BY 4.0).
- **Contents**:
  - Genuine speech: 107 human speakers from the VCTK corpus recorded in an anechoic chamber.
  - Spoofed speech: Generated by 17 speech synthesis and voice conversion systems (TTS algorithms: Tacotron, WaveNet, neural vocoders; VC algorithms: cycle-consistent GANs, spectral mapping).
- **Splits & Usage**:
  - Train: 25,380 utterances (20 speakers: 8 male, 12 female).
  - Dev: 24,844 utterances (10 speakers: 4 male, 6 female).
  - Eval: 71,196 utterances (48 speakers: 21 male, 27 female).
- **Speaker Disjointness Guarantee**: Enforced by protocol and confirmed via `scripts/prepare_datasets.py`. No speaker appears in more than one partition.

### 1.2 In-the-Wild Deepfake Dataset
- **Source**: Fraunhofer AISEC & Ludwig Maximilian University of Munich.
- **Reference**: Müller et al., *"Does Audio Deepfake Detection Generalize to Real-World Scenarios?"*, ACM MMSys 2022.
- **License**: Research-use academic license.
- **Contents**: 31,779 audio samples extracted from publicly available videos and podcasts, featuring 54 political and celebrity figures.
- **Role in VoiceGuard**: Strictly reserved for Condition C2 out-of-domain evaluation. **Never used for model training or parameter tuning.**

## 2. Acoustic Reference & Augmentation Corpora

### 2.1 Mozilla Common Voice (v17.0)
- **Source**: Mozilla Foundation.
- **License**: Creative Commons Zero (CC0).
- **Usage**: Provides natural, uncompressed bona fide human speech diversity across English, Hindi, Bengali, and Tamil to establish physiological acoustic baselines for the challenge verifier.

### 2.2 MUSAN Corpus
- **Source**: Johns Hopkins University (Snyder et al., 2015).
- **License**: Creative Commons Attribution 4.0 International (CC BY 4.0).
- **Contents**: Approximately 109 hours of background noise (technical noise, babble speech, environmental sounds).
- **Usage**: Additive noise augmentation during training (`backend/ai/acoustic/train.py`) and Condition C4 robustness testing.

## 3. Linguistic Scam-Intent Dialogue Dataset

### 3.1 Dataset Construction & Methodology
Because genuine recorded scam calls contain private personal identifying information (PII) and are legally sensitive to distribute, VoiceGuard constructed a multi-turn synthetic scam dialogue corpus:
- **Seed Data**: Public case studies, advisory bulletins, and transcript fragments published by the Consumer Financial Protection Bureau (CFPB), Federal Trade Commission (FTC), and India's Computer Emergency Response Team (CERT-In).
- **Tactic Categories (8 Discrete Labels)**:
  1. `IMPERSONATION_AUTHORITY` (Law enforcement, tax department, banking officers).
  2. `ARTIFICIAL_URGENCY` (Immediate arrest, impending account suspension within 1 hour).
  3. `FINANCIAL_COERCION` (Wire transfer, gift cards, cryptocurrency ATM withdrawal).
  4. `ISOLATION_TACTIC` (Do not hang up, do not discuss with family or bank teller).
  5. `INFORMATION_HARVESTING` (OTP, debit card CVV, national identification numbers).
  6. `EMOTIONAL_EXTORTION` (Kidnapping, medical emergency of a family member).
  7. `ADVANCE_FEE_FRAUD` (Lottery processing fee, customs clearance).
  8. `REMOTE_ACCESS` (Instruction to install AnyDesk, TeamViewer, QuickSupport).
- **Multilingual Scope**: Transcripts balanced across English (5,000), Hindi (4,000), Bengali (1,500), and Tamil (1,500).

## 4. Preprocessing & Canonicalization Pipeline

All audio ingestion follows strict non-lossy canonicalization:
1. **Container Decoding**: Handled via `ai/audio/io.py` using soundfile/librosa/ffmpeg.
2. **Channel Downmix**: Multi-channel tracks averaged to single-channel mono.
3. **Resampling**: Polyphase anti-aliased sinc interpolation to exactly 16,000 Hz.
4. **Sample Encoding**: Signed 16-bit linear PCM (WAV container).
5. **Quality Gating**: Samples failing VAD speech ratio (≥40%), SNR (≥12 dB), clipping (≤1%), or DC offset (≤0.05) are flagged and prevented from biasing downstream layers.

---

## 5. Ethical & Privacy Considerations

- **PII Scrubbing**: All synthesized text scripts are generated without real names, phone numbers, or account numbers.
- **No Biometric Identification**: VoiceGuard models predict synthesis probability and intent tactics. They do not enroll or authenticate speaker identities or create biometric voiceprints.
- **Model Bias Mitigation**: Acoustic datasets feature balanced gender representations (55% female, 45% male across ASVspoof 2019 partitions).
